# The Last Line

## 1. INT. SMALL OFFICE - NIGHT

> The last question lands and he cannot stay to answer it.

One table, two chairs. Rain on the window.

**INTERVIEWER**\
*(quietly)*\
Was it worth it?

The filmmaker stands.\
He leaves the door open.

*CUT TO BLACK.*

## 2. INT. SMALL OFFICE - DAWN

> He returns to the question he left unanswered.

Grey light. The chair is still pushed back.

**FILMMAKER**\
It was.
